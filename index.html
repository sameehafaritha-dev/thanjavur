Peak Season

Winter (October – March)

20°C – 28°C

Pleasant breezes and comfortable sunshine make winter ideal for walking tours, temple visits, and exploring outdoor monuments. Coincides with the Tyagaraja Aradhana (Jan) and Pongal.

Lush Delta

Monsoon (July – September)

24°C – 32°C

The Kaveri delta flourishes with rich emerald paddy fields and full river channels. Intermittent refreshing showers bring scenic temple courtyard reflections and cooler evenings.

Festival Month

Summer (April – June)

30°C – 39°C

Warm tropical weather. Perfect for early morning (6:00 AM – 9:00 AM) and sunset tours. Features the grand Chithirai Big Temple Car (Ther) festival drawing thousands of devotees.

Temple Dress Code

Traditional and modest attire is mandatory inside active temple precincts. Dhotis/formal trousers for men and sarees/salwars for women are standard. Shorts and sleeveless garments are prohibited.

Footwear Depositories

Footwear must be deposited before entering the granite cloisters. Official free footwear counters with tokens are stationed right near the main eastern entrance of the Big Temple.

Photography Guidelines

Photography is permitted in the open courtyards, outer gardens, and palace grounds. Photography inside the inner sanctum sanctorum (garbhagriha) of temples is strictly prohibited.
